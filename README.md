# Titan Reforged CN Wiki

A community wiki for English speaking players on the Chinese World of Warcraft server Titan Reforged (TRR), operated by NetEase.

It is built from our Discord logs and the community setup guide, and covers everything a new or returning player needs. Account setup, the launcher, forcing the English client, game time, addons, voice comms, a CN to EN dictionary, Warcraft Logs parses, and where to find servers, tools, and streamers.

Built with the [Just the Docs](https://just-the-docs.github.io/just-the-docs/) Jekyll theme and published to GitHub Pages.

## Pages

- Getting Started: account, Battle.net China region, login and verification, English client, game time and Alipay
- AddOns: NewBeeBox and WCLBox, BiaoGe (BGLite), MeetingHorn, TranslateCN, and what breaks on CN
- Voice Comms: Discord, YY and YY-Green, GearUP voice routing, Voov for events
- Chinese 101: CN to EN dictionary for dungeons, classes, roles, LFG, and slang
- Parses and Warcraft Logs: the mandatory opt in and ranking on titan.warcraftlogs.com
- Where to Find Things: servers and phases, websites, tools, streamers
- Community: TRR Info Center, Guest Gamers, events, guild rules, staying safe

## Local preview

```bash
bundle install
bundle exec jekyll serve
```

Then open http://localhost:4000.
