# Parses and Warcraft Logs

Titan Reforged has its own Warcraft Logs site: [titan.warcraftlogs.com](https://titan.warcraftlogs.com/). WCL supports TRR but did not make a dedicated Discord channel, so use the TRR one. This page is the "make my logs show up and not be anonymous" guide.

## Mandatory opt in, do this first

Because everyone is registering fresh CN accounts, third party combat info access is off by default. If you skip this:

- Your character is not searchable on Warcraft Logs.
- You are anonymous on every leaderboard.
- Any logs you upload are anonymous too.

Fix it once.

1. Go to [https://wow.blizzard.cn/info-cfg/](https://wow.blizzard.cn/info-cfg/)
2. Log in with your NetEase account.
3. Allow third party access to your account's combat information (toggle it on).

That is it. Do it now, before your first raid.

## How parsing and speedruns work on TRR

The community takes parses seriously. If you have what it takes, there are 7+ hour parse raids available. Ask around.

Updates on WCL ranking rules are usually posted in the Discord.
