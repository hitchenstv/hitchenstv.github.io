# Voice Comms

Two layers. Discord is home base for the English speaking community, and YY is what Chinese raids actually run on. Here is how both work when you are connecting from outside China.

## Discord, home base

This is where coordination, LFG, help, and event sign ups happen.

- TRR Info Center: [discord.gg/YMzFp2MqWN](https://discord.gg/YMzFp2MqWN)
- Secondary invite, announcements: [discord.gg/xsr3dQUCCw](https://discord.gg/xsr3dQUCCw)

Use the channels as intended, react for roles, and ping in the help channels when you are stuck.

## YY, the CN raid voice

Chinese raids almost universally use YY (YY语音, also called YY). A few things to know as a foreigner.

The big one. You can hear YY without a VPN, but to talk you normally need a Chinese IP. This is where the booster comes in, see below.

### Getting a YY account

The [website](https://aq.yy.com/p/reg/mobile.do?fromadv=lgn&appid=1) allows foreign phone numbers to register. It is all in Chinese, but you only do it once. If registration doesn't go through, try after midnight chinese times. There are rate-limits on foreign account registrations.

Some pugs, especially MC GDKPs, require you to be on voice. Be upfront with the raid leader, tell them you are a foreigner but you play well. Most are happy to have you. CN GDKP communities are really welcoming.

### Live translation if you cannot YY

If you want CN voice turned into English text, try TranscriptionSuite (homelab-00 on GitHub, app at [transcriptionsuite.app](https://transcriptionsuite.app/)). It runs CN voice to English text, and you can self host it.

## Quick start

- In the Discord: [TRR Info Center](https://discord.gg/YMzFp2MqWN)
- Grab GearUP (trial is free) and enable YY routing for Titan Reforged
- Make a [YY account](https://aq.yy.com/p/reg/mobile.do?fromadv=lgn&appid=1)
- Tell your RL you are foreign (or don't, they can usually tell), get invited, copy the YY number pasted in /ra, get on voice
- Optional, point TranscriptionSuite at the YY audio so you can read translated comms
