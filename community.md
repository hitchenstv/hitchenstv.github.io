# Community

Titan Reforged's English speaking side is small small but welcoming! 

## TRR Info Center

The TRR Info Center is the main hub for Western players on Chinese servers. Setup guides, announcements, LFG, addon help, parses, and general chaos. It was pulled together by community organizer pit, who personally onboarded the first wave of overseas players.

Join the Discord here: [TRR Info Center](https://discord.gg/YMzFp2MqWN)
